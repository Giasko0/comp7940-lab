## 5. Course Learning Outcomes & Expectations
### Key Concepts Covered
* [cite_start]Distributed system models and computer clusters[cite: 98].
* [cite_start]Virtualization and data centers[cite: 98].
* [cite_start]Cloud computing models, architectures, and mechanisms[cite: 98].
* [cite_start]NIST definitions of Cloud: On-demand self-service, Broad network access, Resource pooling, Rapid elasticity, and Measured service[cite: 84, 85, 87, 91, 92, 94].

### Practical Experience
Students will gain hands-on experience with:
* [cite_start]Git/GitHub[cite: 100].
* [cite_start]Container Technology (Docker)[cite: 100].
* [cite_start]Public Cloud Management and Service consumption (including ChatGPT API)[cite: 100, 102].
* [cite_start]**Note**: While Python is used, it is not the focus; there are no Python-specific questions in the exams[cite: 101].
